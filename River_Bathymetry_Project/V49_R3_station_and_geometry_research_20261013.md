**V49 R3 — station observations and surveyed geometry (13 October 2026)**

Result: **one newly accessible local survey drawing**, covering a Phú Sen sandbar on the lower Đà Rằng/Ba; **zero newly obtained 2021 event hourly water-level samples**, **zero verified 2021 station-coordinate/vertical-datum packages**, and **zero downloadable complete wetted-channel XYZ/cross-section datasets**. Research was performed directly without descendants. This pass extends the existing V49 evidence recap; reservoir catalogues and ZFW were not revisited.

The strongest new geometric evidence is the project-owner EIA published for consultation by the provincial government on 17 March 2026. PDF p.208, drawing I-2, explicitly dates its field map to **January 2024**, covers **9.1419 ha**, and provides a 1:2,000 plan with surveyed spot heights, contours, a coordinate grid, 11 borehole collars and four geological/mineral-reserve transects T.I–T.IV. PDF p.209, I-3, repeats the profiles at horizontal 1:2,000 and vertical 1:200. These are original survey-bearing drawings rather than modeled WSE. [Government publication and attachment](https://snnmt.daklak.gov.vn/lay-y-kien-tham-van-trong-qua-trinh-danh-gia-tac-dong-moi-truong-du-an-khai-thac-mo-cat-vlxdtt-song-da-rang-thon-phu-sen-xa-phu-hoa-1-tinh-dak-lak-3836.html).

Usable printed collar heights, in metres as labeled, are: **LK1 11.71; LK2 13.62; LK3 12.08; LK4 11.73; LK5 13.39; LK6 12.16; LK7 11.88; LK8 11.61; LK9 11.62; LK10 11.20; LK11 11.46**. They support local bar geometry checks or controlled later digitisation, but are not yet datum-compatible model inputs. The inspected drawings do not identify a vertical datum or benchmark tie, and do not provide a paired XYZ table. Borehole penetration depths and the +9 m reserve/excavation boundaries are **not water depths or observed river-bed elevations**. The coverage is a small exposed/semi-exposed bar, not a bank-to-bank bathymetric survey, and the survey postdates the 2021 event. [Original EIA, PDF pp.208–209](https://snnmt.daklak.gov.vn/uploads/sonnmt/ccbvmt/0%20.DTM%20Phu%20Sen%20Binh%20An.pdf).

The main drawing states **VN-2000, central meridian 108°30′, 3° zone**. Its alternative 6° table states 111°, whereas report p.31 labels that table 108°30′; point A also differs (northing 1437932.48 on the drawing versus 1437932.00 in the report). These source inconsistencies remain unresolved. No coordinate transformation or vertical-datum inference was made.
